# Signature moves (approved; animated in the drafts, not in the game yet)

Every monster keeps a few shared basics (bite, sweep, spit and so on) and gets one or two moves of its own, taken from its legend.
Each entry: the move, what it does, how you beat it. "Dodge" means the normal dodge timing; "hits" are separate dodge prompts.

## Dragons
- **Fernwing** (heraldic wyvern). *Leafstorm*: beats its leaf wings and throws a gust of razor leaves, 3 hits. Dodge each. *Taproot*: plants its feet and heals a little over 3 s. Hit it hard to break the heal.
- **Emberwing** (Fafnir). *Dragonfire*: a long sweeping flame breath, 4 hits, burns. Dodge each. *Hoard Rage*: when low, roars and its next 3 attacks come faster.
- **Rimewing** (frost wyrm). *Rime Gale*: one wing beat that freezes on hit. Dodge it. *Icicle Rain*: shakes icicles off its wings, 5 quick hits.
- **Voltwing** (amphiptere). *Coil Lightning*: wraps itself in its wings and discharges, 2 hits, paralyses. *Sky Strafe*: flies off screen and dives across, 1 heavy hit with a long warning.
- **Trimaw** (Zmey Gorynych). *Three Fires*: all three heads breathe at once, left, middle, right in a fast sequence, 3 hits. *Head Snap*: the side heads bite in turn, 2 hits. Breaking a head drops one hit from both.
- **Stormpearl** (Chinese lung). *Pearl Storm*: spins its pearl and calls lightning down, 4 hits, paralyses. *Cloud Ride*: vanishes into its clouds and reappears with a bite.
- **Aurorawing** (feathered serpent). *Plume Veil*: spreads its wings and blinds you with light; the next attack's warning is shorter. *Coil Crush*: rears and slams its coils, 1 heavy hit.
- **Skyrender** (Bahamut). *Judgement*: four wings open, a thunder pillar, 1 huge hit with a very long warning. *Crown Bolts*: 3 bolts from its crown.
- **The Hollow Star** (Tiamat). Keeps Starfall, Star Beam, Comet, Nova and Eclipse.

## Beasts
- **Tuskhide** (Calydonian boar). *Bristle Rush*: a charge that doesn't stop, 2 heavy hits. *Root Out*: tusks rip up the ground, throws rocks.
- **Thornback** (Tarasque). *Shell Spin*: tucks into its thorned shell and spins, 3 hits. *Sting Tail*: a poison sting from behind its shell.
- **Cinderhoof** (bronze bull). *Bronze Charge*: lowers its horns and charges, 1 heavy hit, burns. *Smoke Snort*: blows smoke; the next attack has no warning flash, only sound.
- **Frostmaw** (yeti). *Avalanche Slam*: both fists, 2 heavy hits, freezes. *Snowball*: throws a huge snowball, big projectile.
- **Icetusk** (mammoth). *Trunk Toss*: grabs you with its trunk. Mash to break free or take a heavy hit. *Tusk Sweep*: 2 hits.
- **Ruinhorn** (Karkadann). *Rune Charge*: its runes flare and it charges through, 1 huge hit. *Stone Quake*: stomps, ring of rubble, 2 hits.
- **Gloomfang** (Fenrir). *Shadow Howl*: howls, and two shadow wolves rush you one after another, 2 hits. *Bind Breaker*: when low, it breaks loose and doubles its speed for a while.

## Shelled ones
- **Mudclaw** (Karkinos). *Crusher Grip*: grabs you in its crusher. Mash to escape. *Mud Bubbles*: 3 slowing bubbles.
- **Shellsnap** (hermit crab). *Shell Bunker*: hides in its conch and takes almost no damage for 3 s, then bursts out. *Conch Cannon*: blasts a jet of water through its spiral conch, 2 hits, soaks.
- **Venomsting** (giant scorpion). *Pin and Sting*: pincers pin you, then the sting, 2 hits, poisons. *Tail Barrage*: 4 fast stings.
- **Magmastrider** (spider crab). *Stilt Stomp*: its long legs stamp in a line toward you, 4 hits. *Vent Burst*: its shell vents erupt, burns.
- **Crystalback** (horseshoe crab). *Crystal Shards*: shakes off crystals that ring the ground, 3 hits. *Telson Flip*: flips its tail spike, 1 hit.
- **Duskhorn** (Ushi-oni). *Web Snare*: spits a web; if it hits, you're stuck for a moment. *Oni Gore*: horns and scythe, 2 hits.
- **Pyreclaw** (flame crab). *Pyre Crown*: its flames flare to a pillar, 2 heavy hits, burns. *Ember Claw*: a flaming crusher swing.

## Serpents
- **Tidecoil** (sea serpent). *Whirlpool*: coils and spins the water, 3 hits, soaks. *Breach*: dives and bursts up beneath you.
- **Bogcrown** (basilisk). *Petrifying Glare*: a long stare; if you don't dodge in time you are slowed for a while. *Crowned Strike*: a fast fang strike, poisons.
- **Sandmaw** (death worm). *Burrow*: sinks under the sand; a moving mound shows where it is, then it bursts up, 1 heavy hit. *Acid Spray*: 2 hits.
- **Mirefang** (Hydra). *Many Heads*: the heads bite in a chain, one hit per head still attached. *Regrowth*: a broken head regrows after a while unless you keep hitting the stump.
- **Abysscoil** (angler serpent). *Lure*: dims everything but its lure, then strikes from the dark with a short warning. *Abyss Pull*: a current that drags you closer.
- **Tidereaver** (Leviathan). *Tidal Wave*: a huge wave, 1 heavy hit with a long warning. *Blade Fins*: a spinning slash with its glowing fins, 2 hits.

## Birds
- **Quillbeak** (Stymphalian bird). *Bronze Volley*: fires its feathers, 5 quick hits. *Spear Beak*: one fast stab.
- **Dustspur** (cockatrice). *Stone Gaze*: like the basilisk's glare but shorter, slows. *Spur Kick*: 2 kicks.
- **Gusthawk** (Thunderbird). *Thunderclap*: claps its wings, a lightning ring, 1 heavy hit, paralyses. *Storm Wing*: 3 bolts follow you.
- **Plumecrest** (fenghuang). *Rebirth*: once a hunt, when low, it bursts into light and heals a part of its health. *Streamer Lash*: its tail streamers whip, 3 hits.
- **Dreadbeak** (griffin). *Griffin Dive*: soars off and dives with talons, 1 heavy hit. *Lion Pounce*: 2 hits.
- **Tempestbeak** (roc). *Roc Grab*: snatches you up and drops you. Mash to break free. *Tempest*: wing beats that call a storm, 4 hits.
